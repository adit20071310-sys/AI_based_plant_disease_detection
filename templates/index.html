GENERAL PREVENTION TIPS

Take care of your own Plant

Stop disease before it starts

Smart agronomy practices reduce disease pressure by up to 80%. Start with the basics.

🌱

Use disease-free seeds

Source certified, pathogen-free seeds and seedlings from trusted suppliers.

💧

Avoid overwatering

Excess moisture invites fungal growth. Water deeply but less frequently.

🌬️

Improve air circulation

Space plants generously and prune to keep canopies open and dry.

🔍

Inspect leaves regularly

Weekly scouting catches outbreaks before they spread across the field.

✂️

Remove infected leaves

Prune and destroy diseased tissue — never compost it back into the field.

🧪

Use recommended treatments

Apply organic or labelled fungicides only when disease pressure is confirmed.

🔄

Practice crop rotation

Rotate families every 2–3 seasons to break disease cycles in the soil.

☀️

Sun & sanitation

Clean tools and stakes — sun exposure reduces pathogen survival on surfaces.
